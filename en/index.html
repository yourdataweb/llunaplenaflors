The shop

A neighbourhood florist that does weddings

Lluna Plena Flors is a small shop where the flowers are chosen by hand every week and every order is discussed one to one. Carmen and the team go from a Tuesday bouquet to the whole arch of a ceremony.

They work with seasonal flowers and with whatever the market has at its best that day, which is why the catalogue is not a fixed product list: it is a way of working. If you have an idea, a photo, or just a colour in mind, they tell you what can be done with this week's flowers and what it will cost before anything is started.

Weddings are a large part of the work: bridal bouquets, arches, table centres and all the flowers for the ceremony and the reception. They also make sympathy tributes and wreaths — work where timing rules, and which they take on the same day whenever they can.

How to order

Three steps and it is done

There is no form and no shopping cart: you talk on the phone or come into the shop, and that is how the bouquet comes out the way you want it.

  1. 1

    Call or drop in

    Tell us the occasion, the colour and the budget. A reference photo helps.

  2. 2

    We confirm flowers and price

    We tell you what can be done with this week's flowers and fix the price before making anything.

  3. 3

    Collect it or we deliver

    Pick it up at the shop or we deliver in Vilanova and the villages around it.

Delivery area

Delivery we can promise is within Vilanova i la Geltrú. For the Garraf and Penedès villages we do not promise anything in advance: call us and we will tell you whether we can get there that day.
